## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Partially succeeded — the revision fixed the surface and left the skeleton generic. The palette, Petrona pairing, and vocabulary are genuinely non-transferable, and the four `.subject-mark` equations (`2x + 3 = 11`, `v² = u² + 2as`, `∫ f(x) dx`) are the one compositional element only this product could own. But the *arrangement* is still template, item for item: sticky blurred header, split hero with elevated white checklist card, three-up stat row, eyebrow-with-rule repeated five times, four-step process, 2-up pricing with a badge on the featured card, dark rounded CTA band. Worse, the genericness relocated into the hero: `.hero-stats` presents three non-facts in the visual grammar of metrics — and DESIGN.md's own don't-list names "unlabeled stat row" as the failure mode this system exists to prevent. The only unrepeatable proof the business owns (100% in LC Higher Maths) appears nowhere above the fold.

**Deterministic scan**: `detect.mjs` exit 0 — **clean, 0 findings**. The agent sanity-checked the detector against a deliberately-bad scratch file and confirmed it correctly fires, so the clean result is genuine rather than a silent no-op.

**Important caveat on that clean result**: it is much weaker evidence than it looks, for two reasons. (1) The detector has no contrast checking — all four AA failures below were invisible to it. (2) Earlier this session DESIGN.md's type ramp was widened to document whatever the code already used, which structurally blunted the `design-system-font-size` rule's ability to catch drift. A manual set-difference by the evidence pass still found `0.82rem` and `0.86rem` used in code but absent from the frontmatter — a genuine disagreement between the detector and a direct token comparison. Treat "detector clean" as "no mechanical slop patterns", not "design-system compliant".

**Visual overlays**: unavailable — no browser automation tool exposed in this session (fallback signal). All layout and contrast findings are derived from source reading plus independently recomputed WCAG ratios.

## Overall Impression

The content and voice are genuinely strong; the *system* has drifted out from under them. Two things are actively costing conversions right now: the most important button on the page (contact-band "Email me") fails AA contrast at 2.94:1, and the Subjects tabs default to Junior Cycle — so a skimming visitor sees a site that appears to teach Maths only, while Applied Maths and Physics (the entire stated differentiator) sit behind clicks. The single biggest opportunity is to stop hiding content that persuades and start showing proof that converts: real credentials above the fold, all four levels visible at once.

## What's Working

1. **The Irish-native vocabulary layer** ("grinds", Junior Cycle, 7 H1s, TY, Ordinary & Higher, euro pricing). It functions as tacit proof — a parent reads it and knows the author is inside the system, not selling into it. That does the job PRODUCT.md forbids testimonials from doing.
2. **The header's deliberate CTA restraint.** `.nav-whatsapp` is a teal outline that inverts on hover rather than a filled terracotta button. DESIGN.md argues the reasoning explicitly and the build honors it — the one place the One Accent Rule is actively defended.
3. **The `.subject-mark` equations.** A subject signal disguised as ornament: `v² = u² + 2as` tells a Leaving Cert student "this is Applied Maths" faster than the tag row beneath it.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Contact-band primary CTA fails AA contrast at 2.94:1** *(independently verified)*
- **Why it matters**: `.contact .btn-primary` is `#c98a26` gold inheriting white text — 2.94:1 against a 4.5:1 requirement. Its hover state is 3.65:1, also failing. This is the "Email me" button, the single most important pixel on a Persuade surface, and it is the least legible CTA on the page in sunlight or on a dim phone. `.format-card .perk` ("Every 8th session is free") has the identical 2.94:1 problem — the most reassuring sentence in the pricing block is the hardest to read.
- **Fix**: darken to ≈`#9a6a1c` (white on that ≈4.6:1), or flip to a white fill with `--deep-teal` text (10.4:1).
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

**[P0] Default tab hides Applied Maths and Physics from every non-interacting visitor**
- **Why it matters**: `#tab-jc` is `checked`, so a skimmer sees one sentence about Junior Cycle Maths and a single `Maths` tag. The subjects named in the page title, the H1, and PRODUCT.md's positioning are three clicks away. The highest-value segment (Leaving Cert — the only level carrying all three subjects) lands on the least relevant panel. All four panels combined are four sentences and seven tags; the interaction machinery costs more than the payload.
- **Fix**: drop the tabs for a single four-row level→subjects table or 2×2 grid — everything visible, zero interaction. That alone clears four of the six cognitive-load failures. If tabs stay, default to Leaving Cert and add proper `role="tablist"/"tab"/"tabpanel"` semantics.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

**[P1] The system's most-repeated label token fails contrast in 3 of its 5 placements** *(independently verified)*
- **Why it matters**: `.eyebrow` is `#bf4d28` at 12.5px bold — not large text, so it needs 4.5:1. On soft cream it computes to 4.22:1; on the hero wash 4.01:1. Only the two on cream clear it, at 4.71:1.
- **Fix**: darken eyebrows to ≈`#a8421f`, or switch them to `--deep-teal` on soft-cream sections.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable audit`

**[P1] The hero spends its most valuable real estate on non-facts**
- **Why it matters**: `.hero-stats` shows "1-1 & Group", "100% Online", "Junior Cycle–Undergrad" — all three restate the eyebrow, the lead, and the card beside them. Meanwhile "100% in LC Higher Maths", "7 H1s", and "TCD Foundation Scholar" — the only unfabricated proof this business owns — sit ~900px down the page. This is also the surviving SaaS-template tell DESIGN.md names by hand.
- **Fix**: replace the three pseudo-stats with the real ones so the numerals are actual numerals and credibility lands above the fold.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable bolder`

**[P2] Mobile nav links are ~23px touch targets and the panel is mispositioned**
- **Why it matters**: stacked bare `<a>` elements with `gap:18px` and no vertical padding give ≈23.5px hit areas — roughly half the 44px minimum. The panel is `top:64px` while the header measures ≈67.5px, so its first few pixels tuck behind the sticky header and bleed through the backdrop filter.
- **Fix**: `padding:12px 0` on the links; `top:100%` instead of a hardcoded 64px.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

**[P2] Pricing cards don't align, and terracotta prices cancel the featured tier**
- **Why it matters**: `.perk` exists only on the featured card and the group `.fit-line` wraps to three lines, so both bullet lists and both CTAs sit at different heights — in the one section where alignment matters most. And `.price` colors *both* €40 and €15 rust, so the accent no longer distinguishes the featured card from the other one.
- **Fix**: flex-column the cards with `margin-top:auto` on the CTA; keep rust on €40 only, set €15 in `--deep-teal`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

## Design-System Adherence

DESIGN.md is no longer governing the build:

- **The One Accent Rule — violated badly.** The Formats viewport shows six simultaneous terracotta marks (eyebrow, featured border, badge, both prices, primary button). Because `.price` colors both cards, the accent no longer marks the featured tier at all. `#how` adds five more (four step numerals + eyebrow).
- **Card token contradicted by 100% of cards.** The frontmatter and prose both specify soft-cream card backgrounds; every card in the build is `#fff`.
- **"No pure white anywhere" broken in three places** — the hero gradient terminates at `#ffffff` instead of `var(--bg)`, creating a hard tonal step into the next section; plus the mobile nav panel and `.hero-card`.
- **Lift-on-Purpose half-abandoned** — `.subject-tabs` has no shadow and no hover lift.
- **The Italic Accent Rule contradicts its own frontmatter** — the prose reserves Petrona italic for the logo and step numerals, but the `formula` token specifies italic for exactly the `.subject-mark` use the prose forbids. The prose needs amending, not the code.
- **Font-stack drift** — every declaration is `'Petrona',serif`; Georgia was dropped from the documented stack, so a Google Fonts failure falls straight to Times.
- **Stale doc** — `monogram` (3.4rem), `title-sm`, `rounded.md`, and the "subject-card icon backgrounds" prose all describe components that no longer exist.

## Cognitive Load

**6 of 8 items fail**: single focus (three competing above-fold actions, two destinations for one intent), visual hierarchy (the only shadowed object pulls the eye off the H1; identical prices cancel the featured tier), one-thing-at-a-time (tabs demand self-classification before delivering any information), minimal choices (5 nav items, 5 credential tags with two overlapping), working memory (tier comparison across misaligned cards), progressive disclosure (applied backwards — hides 55 words of persuasion while omitting the information that actually blocks a €40/hr purchase). Chunking and grouping pass cleanly.

## Emotional Journey

The peak is unambiguously the About section — real photo, "100% in Higher Level Maths", the windsurfing-instructor line — and placing it second was a good instinct. But the opening is emotionally flat (three non-facts, zero credibility above the fold), and there's a real valley at Formats: a cost-anxious parent hits €40/hour with no answer to "how long is a session", "what if it isn't working", or "what happens in the free intro chat" — and the one reassuring line that exists is the least legible text in the block. The close ("Ready to get started?") is the most replaceable copy on the page, so the peak-end pair is a strong middle followed by a forgettable ending.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer)**: Reads "Maths, Applied Maths & Physics" in the H1, scrolls to Subjects, sees only Junior Cycle Maths — and concludes the site doesn't cover the Applied Maths they're sitting in 6th year. If they get past that, the CTA path is incoherent: header says "Get in touch" → WhatsApp, hero says "Book a free intro chat" → a section, that section's primary button says "Email me". Three labels, three mechanisms, one intent. No answer anywhere to session length, platform, or what the intro chat involves.

**Casey (Distracted Mobile)**: On a 375px phone the four subject tabs measure ~513px against ~325px available, so they wrap to two rows — and because each tab carries `border-bottom:3px` with `margin-bottom:-1px`, a selected tab on row one renders its rust underline floating mid-block instead of joined to the panel. There is not a single `:active` rule in the file, so no tap produces any feedback. Tapping the header WhatsApp link navigates out of the site in the same tab.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent)**: Four structural failures beyond the contrast issues. (1) The hamburger label is `aria-hidden="true"` while the control is a checkbox with `aria-label` — wrong role, no `aria-expanded`, so a disclosure announces as "checkbox, not checked". (2) The subject tabs have no tablist/tab/tabpanel roles, no `aria-selected`, no `aria-controls`; panels are unlabelled and unassociated with the radios revealing them. (3) `scroll-behavior:smooth` with no `prefers-reduced-motion` guard anywhere. (4) No skip link, and a heading-order break — `<h1>` is followed by `<h3>What you get</h3>` before any `<h2>`.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the only two facts a visitor remembers are "100% in Leaving Cert Higher Maths" and "€40/hour", why do the most valuable 600 vertical pixels hold three restatements of "online, 1-1 or group" while the 100% sits in paragraph two of section two?
2. The tabs hide ~55 words behind four clicks, while the questions that actually block a €40/hr purchase — session length, platform, cancellation, what the free intro chat involves — aren't on the page at all. Is progressive disclosure pointed at the right content?
