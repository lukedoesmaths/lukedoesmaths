# Design System: LukeDoesMaths

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Luke Does Maths"**

The system is named plainly after the brand itself: no borrowed metaphor, no aesthetic pretense — the design should read as direct and personal as the name. The mood is warm, precise, and unhurried. Nothing on the page rushes the visitor toward a decision; typography, color, and pacing all signal that the person behind this site is calm, capable, and has nothing to prove through visual noise. Credentials speak for themselves; the design doesn't need to shout alongside them.

The palette is warm-editorial (cream paper tones, terracotta, deep teal, gold) rather than cool-corporate. Layout is generous and airy — full-width sections with consistent breathing room — never dense or app-like. The one confirmed anti-reference: this must not read as a generic SaaS/startup template. Every recurring pattern (card grids, eyebrow labels, pricing tiers) needs enough specific, warm detail that it couldn't be mistaken for an unrelated product's site with the copy swapped out.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm cream paper backgrounds, never stark white or cool grey
- One confident accent (terracotta) used sparingly for action and emphasis; deep teal carries structural/heading weight
- Generous whitespace and large section padding; nothing feels cramped or transactional
- Soft, gentle elevation (light paper-lift shadows), never heavy or glossy
- Warm, approachable interactive states — soft hovers, no jarring transitions

## Colors

The palette is warm and paper-toned throughout: no pure white, no cool grey, no saturated "tech blue" anywhere in the system.

### Primary
- **Warm Terracotta** (#bf4d28): The one confident accent. Drives primary CTAs, the eyebrow labels, hover states on links and outline buttons, the logo's second word, and the "featured" badge. Used sparingly — its rarity is what makes it read as intentional rather than decorative.

### Secondary
- **Chalkboard Teal** (#20443f): Carries structural weight — headings, nav text, the outline-button family, and the closing contact section's background. Named for the classroom association (a nod to the tutoring context) without being literal about it.

### Tertiary
- **Burnished Gold** (#c98a26): The rarest accent, reserved for the checkmark bullets and the primary CTA fill inside the dark contact band (where terracotta would clash against chalkboard teal). Used as a **fill only, never as text on white** — gold text on a light surface cannot reach AA (2.94:1). The contact CTA sets Ink text on this fill (5.38:1).
- **Burnished Gold Deep** (#8f6119): The text-safe gold. Any gold that has to be *read* rather than filled uses this — the "Every 8th session is free" perk line and the Transition Year equation mark (5.40:1 on white).
- **Burnished Gold Light** (#dda63f): Hover fill for the contact-band CTA. The button brightens rather than emptying, so the primary action never loses prominence at the moment of intent.

### Accent variants
- **Terracotta Deep** (#a8421f): Carries terracotta wherever the standard accent cannot hold AA or needs to intensify — all eyebrow labels (4.98:1 on the hero wash, 5.24:1 on soft cream, where plain terracotta failed at 4.01 and 4.22) and the primary-button hover fill (6.06:1 with white).

### Neutral
- **Ink** (#26221d): Primary body text color; warm near-black, never pure black.
- **Muted** (#6f695f): Secondary/supporting text — descriptions, captions, fine print.
- **Cream Paper** (#fdfbf6): Page background.
- **Soft Cream** (#f4eee1): Alternate section background, used to separate "How it works" and "About" from the surrounding white-cream sections without a hard border.
- **Sand Border** (#e5dcc7): All hairline borders and dividers.
- **Terracotta Light** (#f4e7d7): The hero's gradient wash — a tint of the primary accent, not an independent color.
- **White** (#ffffff): Text and icon color on filled/dark surfaces (primary buttons, badges, the contact band, card backgrounds for pricing tiers). Never used as a page or section background — that's always Cream Paper or Soft Cream.
- **Header Glass** (rgba(255,255,255,0.9)): The translucent backing behind the blurred sticky nav; white lets the page content show through faintly as it scrolls beneath.
- **Contact Body** (#d6e2dd): The paragraph text color inside the dark Chalkboard Teal contact band — a pale, desaturated teal-white chosen for readability without the harshness of pure white on a dark surface.
- **Contact Border** (rgba(255,255,255,0.4)): The outline-button border inside the contact band, where a solid neutral border would be too heavy against the dark background.

### Named Rules
**The Read-It-Or-Fill-It Rule.** Every accent has a fill value and a text value, and they are not interchangeable. Gold and terracotta at full strength are fill colors; when the same accent must be *read* as text on a light surface, it steps to its `-deep` variant. No accent is ever placed as text on a background it cannot clear 4.5:1 against.

**The One Accent Rule.** Warm Terracotta is the only color that ever fills a primary call-to-action outside the dark contact band. If a new component needs a second "loud" color, it's a sign the composition needs rethinking, not a new token.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Petrona (with Georgia, serif fallback)
**Body Font:** Work Sans (with system-ui, sans-serif fallback)

**Character:** Petrona is a warm, moderately quirky serif that carries the display/heading voice and the site's few italic flourishes (the logo mark, step numerals); Work Sans stays neutral and highly legible for everything a visitor actually has to read closely. The pairing reads editorial-but-approachable rather than corporate or purely academic.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, 2.9rem desktop / 2.1rem mobile, 1.14 line-height): Hero H1 only.
- **Headline** (600, 2rem, 1.2 line-height): Section H2s ("What I teach", "How it works", etc.).
- **Title** (600, 1.15–1.3rem): Card and component headings (H3s).
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.6 line-height): All paragraph copy; comfortable reading measure, never justified.
- **Label** (700, 0.78–0.92rem, uppercase, 0.04–0.12em letter-spacing): Eyebrows, nav links, button text, tags.

The full step list (all sizes actually in use, frontmatter is the normative source): 0.72rem (badge) · 0.75rem (icon-glyph) · 0.76rem (caption/tag) · 0.78rem (eyebrow) · 0.82rem (tab-label-sm: subject tabs under 640px) · 0.85rem (label) · 0.86rem (fit-line) · 0.9rem (body-sm) · 0.92rem (label-sm: buttons, nav links) · 0.93rem (body-list-sm) · 0.95rem (body-list) · 1rem (body) · 1.1rem (subtitle) · 1.2rem (step-num) · 1.3rem (title) · 1.35rem (logo) · 1.4rem (formula: the Subjects equation marks) · 1.5rem (stat) · 1.6rem (menu-glyph, decorative UI glyph not prose) · 1.8rem (headline-sm) · 2rem (headline) · 2.1rem (display-mobile) · 2.2rem (price-display) · 2.9rem (display).

### Named Rules
**The Italic Accent Rule.** Petrona's italic is reserved for small, personal touches and for mathematical notation — the logo mark, the "How it works" step numerals, and the Subjects equation marks (the `formula` token), where italic is the correct convention for algebraic variables rather than decoration. Never for body copy or for headings at large sizes, where it would read as overwrought.

## Layout

A single centered container (max-width 1120px, 24px side padding) holds every section. Sections use generous vertical rhythm: 80px top/bottom padding on desktop, dropping to 56px under 640px. Content grids step down predictably at three widths (850px for 2/3-column grids, 800px for the About split, 750px for the pricing pair) rather than one blanket mobile breakpoint, so each section collapses at the point its own content actually needs it. The overall feel is airy and unhurried — nothing is dense or dashboard-like.

**Documented exception:** "How it works" (`#how`) intentionally breaks the standard rhythm (48px/32px instead of 80px/56px). Its content is four short one-line steps; the generous default padding read as excess whitespace around that little text, so its padding was deliberately tightened rather than padding the copy out to fill the space.

**Documented exception:** Blog post bodies (`.prose`) cap at 720px, narrower than the standard 1120px container. Long-form reading needs a shorter line length than a marketing section; the 1120px container stays for the post's header/footer chrome, but the article text itself steps down inside it.

## Elevation & Depth

Soft, paper-like lift: a single diffuse shadow (`0 6px 18px rgba(38,34,29,0.09)`) shared by the hero card, subject cards on hover, and the pricing cards. Depth is used sparingly and only on surfaces that visually "sit above" the page — flat sections (nav, footer, section backgrounds) carry no shadow at all; separation there comes from background tone shifts (cream vs. soft-cream) instead.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Paper Lift** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 18px rgba(38,34,29,0.09)`): The only shadow in the system. Used on the hero info card, on subject cards during hover, and permanently on the two pricing cards.

### Named Rules
**The Lift-on-Purpose Rule.** Shadow only appears on surfaces the visitor is meant to read as a discrete, liftable object (a card), never on structural chrome like the header or footer.

## Shapes

Two radius scales doing two different jobs: a tight 3px on every interactive control (buttons, badges, tags) so actions read as precise and clickable, and a generous 10px on containing surfaces (cards, the avatar, the contact band) so content areas read as soft and restful. Borders are hairline (1–2px) and always Sand Border colored, never pure black or grey.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 3px radius, uppercase label, 0.04em letter-spacing.
- **Primary:** Warm Terracotta fill, white text, 13px/28px padding.
- **Hover/Focus:** Primary *deepens* rather than empties — the fill moves to Terracotta Deep and the label stays white. It does not invert to a transparent outline: emptying the fill both dropped the label to 4.01:1 over the hero wash and made the primary action less prominent at the exact moment of intent. A visible 2px terracotta focus ring carries keyboard focus.
- **Outline:** Transparent fill, Chalkboard Teal text/border; hover inverts to solid teal with white text.

### Cards (Subject / Format)
- **Corner Style:** 10px radius.
- **Background:** Soft Cream for subject cards; white for pricing/format cards.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Paper Lift on hover for subject cards; permanent Paper Lift on pricing cards (they're meant to read as chosen/considered objects, not passive content blocks).
- **Border:** 1px Sand Border; the "featured" pricing card upgrades to a 2px Warm Terracotta border plus an overlapping badge.

### Tags / Chips
- **Style:** White or Terracotta-Light background, Sand Border or transparent border, 3px radius, uppercase-free small label text.
- **State:** No interactive state — these are informational, not selectable.

### Navigation
- **Style:** Sticky, translucent-blurred cream header; uppercase Chalkboard Teal labels with Warm Terracotta hover. The header carries a single CTA — a Chalkboard Teal outline "Get in touch" button linking straight to WhatsApp — which inverts to a solid teal fill on hover. The header deliberately holds no filled terracotta CTA: with one action in the bar, the outline treatment keeps the accent rare and reserves terracotta for in-page conversion points.
- **Mobile:** A native disclosure (no JS dependency) drops the nav into a full-width panel below the header; the trigger is a plain hamburger glyph with a visible focus ring.
- **Current-page state:** On non-homepage pages (e.g. blog pages), the nav link for the current page is permanently Warm Terracotta rather than only on hover, with `aria-current="page"` — the one place plain (non-deep) terracotta text is used outside a hover/large-text context, since it's a small, deliberate, singular exception rather than a recurring pattern.

### Blog Post Elements
- **Post Card** (blog listing grid): Same family as the Subject/Format cards — white background, 1px Sand Border, 10px radius — but Paper Lift only on hover, never permanent. Posts are browsable content to skim, not a chosen product like a pricing tier, so they don't carry the pricing cards' permanent-lift weight.
- **Diagram Figure:** Soft Cream background, 1px Sand Border, 10px radius, capped at 440px wide and centered, holding one hand-authored inline SVG plus a caption. Diagram SVGs use the palette's literal hex values directly (not `var()`) since they're static illustrations, not interactive UI needing live theme response. Caption text reuses the existing `caption` type token (0.76rem, Muted) rather than introducing a new size.
- **Worked-Algebra Box** (`.demo`): White background, 4px Warm Terracotta Deep left border, 10px radius. This mirrors the `ldmexample` terracotta convention from the LaTeX student-notes system (`Notes/template/lukedoesmaths.sty`) — terracotta already means "worked example" in the brand's other medium, so the web picks up the same color-to-meaning mapping rather than inventing a new one. The label line uses Terracotta Deep (never plain terracotta) per the Read-It-Or-Fill-It Rule.
- **Closing Callout Box** (`.callout`): Soft Cream background, 4px Chalkboard Teal left border, 10px radius. Mirrors the LaTeX system's `ldmsummary` teal side-rule ("key points" / reminder convention) — used here for the one closing note per post that visual intuition supports but doesn't replace algebraic practice.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep Warm Terracotta rare — one primary action's worth of visual weight per screen, per the One Accent Rule.
- **Do** use Chalkboard Teal for anything structural (headings, nav, outline buttons) rather than introducing a second neutral-dark color.
- **Do** keep every surface warm-toned; no pure white, pure black, or cool grey anywhere in the system.
- **Do** give every card the same 10px/Paper-Lift treatment so the system reads as one family, not a set of one-off components.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a second competing accent color; if a new section needs emphasis, reuse terracotta or gold rather than introducing a new hue.
- **Don't** let the display serif (Petrona) appear at body-text sizes or in long-form copy — it's a heading/accent voice only.
- **Don't** reach for a generic SaaS/startup layout pattern (icon-in-a-tinted-square feature grid, gradient-circle avatar, unlabeled stat row) without adding a detail specific to this brand — that's the exact failure mode the critique flagged and this system exists to avoid.
