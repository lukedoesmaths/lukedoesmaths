# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS (single file, no build step) — confirmed, keep as-is

## Users

Junior Cycle, Leaving Cert (Ordinary & Higher), and first-year undergraduate students needing Maths, Applied Maths, or Physics support — looking to build real understanding and exam confidence, not just pass.

## Product Purpose

1-1 and small-group online grinds (tutoring) in Maths, Applied Maths, and Physics. Success means the student books an intro chat, then ongoing sessions, and improves genuine understanding and exam results.

## Positioning

Taught directly by Luke: 1st Class Honours Theoretical Physics (Trinity College Dublin), TCD Foundation Scholar, 7 H1s in the Leaving Cert including 100% in Higher Level Maths, 5 years tutoring experience. Personal credibility and depth of subject mastery (not a tutoring agency/marketplace) is the differentiator.

## Operating Context

All sessions delivered live online (video call), 1-1 or small group. Contact via email or WhatsApp. No in-person option currently offered.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Subjects by level: Junior Cycle (Maths only), Transition Year (Maths only — keeping skills sharp and bridging into 5th year), Leaving Cert (Maths, Applied Maths, Physics — Ordinary & Higher), Undergraduate (Maths and Physics foundation/first-year modules only — no Applied Maths at undergrad level).
- Formats: 1-1 (fully tailored) or small group (up to 5 students per group, shared cost).
- Pricing is fixed and published: 1-1 grinds €40/hour, with every 8th session free (loyalty perk). Group grinds €15/hour per student.
- Groups are encouraged to form from friends in the same class, so sessions can follow material as it's covered in school.
- No booking/scheduling system integrated — contact is manual via email or WhatsApp.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: LukeDoesMaths.
- Voice: direct, warm, no-pressure, competence-forward.
- Personal detail used as color/differentiation: also a qualified sailing and windsurfing instructor, taught internationally — used to support the "can break down any tricky skill" narrative, not a core service.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, reviews, or student results currently available or authorized for use — do not fabricate any social proof, ratings, or quotes. A real photo of Luke is now in use in the About section (`images/luke.jpg`).

## Product Principles

1. Credibility over hype: let concrete academic/tutoring credentials do the persuading, not generic marketing claims.
2. Frictionless first contact: the intro chat and simple enquiry channels (email/WhatsApp) are the primary conversion goal — every page should point there.
3. Clarity for two decision points: which subject/level fits, and 1-1 vs. group — these should never be ambiguous.
4. No invented proof: absent real testimonials/results, do not simulate them; strengthen the page through the tutor's real, verifiable background instead.
